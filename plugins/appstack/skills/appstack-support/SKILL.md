# Booking a call with Appstack support

Appstack support is reachable two ways: a live call, or a message
(`support@appstack.tech`). Most questions should go to the second — a call is
for the minority of cases where live back-and-forth actually saves time over
async messages.

## Book a call when

- There are **3+ interdependent settings or decisions** to sort out together
  (e.g. a full ad campaign setup).
- **The right answer depends on live data** and would change the
  recommendation (e.g. when to switch optimization events, campaign
  structure) — not a question with one fixed answer.
- It's a **strategy question** that's faster to iterate on live than over
  several back-and-forth messages.

**Signal to watch for:** if you catch yourself writing "it depends, we'd need
to look at it live" more than once in the same conversation, that's the cue
to book a call instead of continuing to message.

## Just message support when

- It's a **factual question with a clear, documented answer**.
- It's **troubleshooting where the cause can be diagnosed** from what's sent
  (screenshots, logs, IDs) — no back-and-forth needed to see it.
- It's **anything product/how-to related**.
